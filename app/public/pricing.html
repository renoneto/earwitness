Pricing

Paid early access, once it opens, runs until the pilot ends. Each plan is one private workspace. The free judges’ pilot plays short audio clips for some ads; paid plans don’t include clips. Plan A goes on sale only if it is ready before the pilot ends.

If ready in time

Ad intelligence

No full transcripts and no transcript search.

$99 a month

What the plan includes

  • Detected ads, brands, shows, campaigns, saved searches, alerts and notes.
