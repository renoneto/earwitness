Coverage

What we cover, how deeply, and what we don’t claim. Every count in the app is about the shows we cover, not all podcasts.

Full coverage

Transcript and a list of ads. Search, alerts, ad lists and campaign checks all use it.

Transcript only

Searchable, and alerts read it. There is no ad list, so ads can’t be checked there.

Which shows

EarWitness covers the shows in its collection. It is not every podcast, and a missing result says nothing about shows outside it.

Episodes with gaps

Some episodes are not screened yet, some were only partly processed, and some have only their title and date. Each is labelled on its page.

Counts cover our shows only

Every count in the app counts what we hold: episodes, passages and ads from the shows we cover. It says nothing about other podcasts.

No result is not absence

A search with no matches means nothing matched in the shows we cover. It does not mean the brand was never mentioned anywhere.

Unknown dates stay visible

Episodes with no known publish date are marked “Date unknown”. When no date filter is on, they still show, with that label.

Later recordings can change

Some ads are inserted when an episode is downloaded, so a later download can hold different ads. A saved passage keeps the transcript version you saved; the current transcript may differ.

What we don’t claim

We don’t report reach, listens, spend, revenue or accuracy scores. A timestamp is where something was heard in one episode file; listeners may hear different ads.

No accuracy numbers

We don’t quote test or accuracy numbers here; past test results are not quality claims. A number in the app counts what we hold, for the filters shown.
