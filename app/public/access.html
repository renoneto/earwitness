Judges’ access

This is a private pilot for the competition judges. You join only with a login that the EarWitness team sends you.

Sign in with your login

Sign in

Use the judge login from your submission.

Sign in with the email address and password from the login the EarWitness team sends you. You can change your password on the Settings page. Forgot your password? Ask support@getearwitness.com for a new one.

Where to start

Try these first: play the real ad on your Overview, or search a brand; compare two brands; set an alert for a word or brand.

What the judges' pilot does not include

  • No public registration or invitations. Paid early access, when it opens, is described on Pricing.
  • No uptime guarantee. For what is kept and for how long, see Privacy.
  • No sharing with anyone outside the workspace.
  • No agent access: AI agents and API tokens that act for you are refused. Ask and meaning alerts are features of EarWitness itself. They do not let any outside agent or token reach your workspace.
  • No billing. Nothing is charged.
  • No adding your own shows or episodes. The EarWitness team’s automatic import adds new episodes each night.
  • No email. EarWitness sends no email.

Audio clips

Some passages that EarWitness flags as ads include a short audio clip, up to about 100 seconds, so you can hear the full detected passage. The app offers no download, export, or sharing controls for clips. Agents cannot open them. They are deleted with the other pilot data.

Privacy facts
