Terms

This is a private pilot for the competition judges. It is free. You join only with a login that the EarWitness team sends you.

What this is

EarWitness finds ads and brand mentions in podcast transcripts and shows the passage behind each one.

  • It runs for about 8 weeks and ends on December 18, 2026. That date does not move. At that time the service stops for good.
  • The service stops for short periods when content is imported or an account is added or removed.

Whose content it is

  • The demo content is public podcast episodes that EarWitness transcribed. It is here only so you can evaluate EarWitness.
  • A show that wants its episodes removed can write to support@getearwitness.com. We remove them.
  • Use it only to evaluate EarWitness. Do not copy, publish or pass on the source material. An export does not give you the right to share it.
  • Each workspace holds one collection under one signed rights record and, for Ask and meaning alerts, one signed addendum. The app allows only the uses they cover.
  • If those rights end early, the whole service stops. The affected content is deleted, with no export.
  • Notes and items you save are stored in the workspace. They are deleted with it.
  • Do not share your sign-in. Do not try to reach another workspace. Report any flaw you find to support@getearwitness.com.

Paid early access

This section applies only if you buy early access. Judges are never charged. Paid early access, when it opens, is described on Pricing.

  • Early access ends on December 18, 2026. Your last month is charged only up to that day. No charge after it. All data is deleted within 60 days after it.
  • Your workspace is ready the morning after payment. We set it up in our nightly maintenance window (01:30 Pacific).
  • One purchase per person.

Using results

A result shows what was said in a specific episode. It is not proof of ad spend, reach or delivery. A search with no result does not prove an ad never ran. Some campaign checks say "Not enough data"; treat those as unknown. Answers from Ask and matches from meaning alerts are written by an AI model and can be wrong. Check each cited passage. Check anything important against the original episode.

Examples on the home page

The ad quote and the screens on the home page come from real episodes in our collection. They show what was heard, not proof that an ad ran as bought. The Ferncastle campaign check is made up to show how EarWitness works and proves nothing about any advertiser.
