Privacy

While the pilot runs, we keep your sign-in ID and email, your sessions, the demo content, and what you create in the workspace. It is hosted on Hetzner Cloud in Nuremberg, Germany. Questions you ask, earlier answers in the same chat, meaning-alert sentences, the passages a word search finds for them, and brand names from the collection are also processed by Meta (Meta's Model API), possibly outside the EU, including in the United States. Meta also gets nearby transcript text when the model asks for it, each passage's show, episode, time and whether it is inside an ad, and the question a brand button asks for you. EarWitness sends no name, email or account ID. Do not type personal information into a question. Our deletion limits cover our own servers, not copies Meta keeps.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Our server stores your password only as a one-way hash, never the password itself. Whenever the EarWitness team sends you a password, change it on the Settings page after you sign in.
  • Service logs can hold account and workspace IDs and network (IP) addresses, for example of a failed sign-in. They never hold transcript text. Service logs are kept 30 days, plus up to 7 days in server backups.
  • After a deletion request, your access ends within 3 days. Other copies on our host, including backups, are gone within 60 days. A few things stay longer; see How to leave.
  • At December 18, 2026, all access ends when the service stops. All pilot data on our host, including backups, is gone within 60 days after that.
  • Your Ask questions and answers, with copies of the passages the search found for them. In the app, only you can see your Ask questions and answers. They are deleted with the workspace.
  • Your meaning alerts: the sentence, the search words, and each check's matches, with the passage and the model's one-line reason. Like other alerts, everyone in the workspace can see them. They are deleted with the workspace.
  • A deletion record stays, during the pilot and after it: request records, removed account IDs and emails, and the signed rights records and addenda. It holds no transcript text. It has no set end date.
  • Deletion removes files and records. It cannot recall files you downloaded or pages already shown to you.

How to leave

  • You can stop at any time. Sign out to end your session.
  • To have your account removed, write to support@getearwitness.com from the email you sign in with.
  • Removal is final for this pilot. A removed account cannot rejoin.
  • We first shut your account out; the deletion itself runs after our safety checks confirm it works. For the time limits, see What we keep, above. Some things stay longer: your account ID next to what you wrote and any email typed into a note or other workspace text, until the workspace is deleted, and the deletion record (also under What we keep).
  • If you want a copy of your results, export them before you ask for removal.

Exports

You can export results. An export can contain ad excerpts and your own notes. It holds no transcript text and no audio. Treat downloaded files as portable copies: handle and share them deliberately.

Outside requests

These pages load only their own text, styles, fonts, script and images, all served by EarWitness. Nothing loads from other sites: no outside fonts, scripts, trackers or images. Show cover art and brand logos are copies we host; they belong to their owners and are shown only to name the show or brand. In the pilot, the app contacts one outside service: Meta's Model API, when you use Ask or a meaning alert. No outside program can connect to your workspace.

Not promised here

  • No zero-retention promise. Deletion follows the limits above.
  • No SOC 2 certification.
